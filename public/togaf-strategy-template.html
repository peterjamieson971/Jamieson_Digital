Big Corp Enterprise IT Strategy

TOGAF Architecture Development Method (ADM) Approach | 2025-2028

Executive Summary

This comprehensive IT strategy follows the TOGAF Architecture Development Method to align technology investments with Big Corp's business objectives. Through systematic analysis of business, information systems, and technology architectures, we have identified key transformation opportunities that will deliver $45M in annual value while reducing technical risk. The strategy emphasizes cloud-native solutions, data-driven decision making, and customer-centric digital experiences, requiring a total investment of $15M over three years with an expected ROI of 280%.

TOGAF ADM Phases Overview

Preliminary Phase

Framework establishment and organizational readiness

Phase A: Architecture Vision

Strategic direction and stakeholder alignment

Phase B: Business Architecture

Business capabilities and process optimization

Phase C: Information Systems

Data and application architecture strategies

Phase D: Technology Architecture

Infrastructure and platform roadmap

Phase E: Opportunities & Solutions

Implementation planning and portfolio definition

Phase F: Migration Planning

Detailed transition roadmap and sequencing

Phase G: Implementation Governance

Project oversight and architecture compliance

Phase H: Architecture Change Management

Continuous monitoring and evolution

Architecture Vision

"To transform Big Corp into a digitally-enabled, data-driven organization that delivers exceptional customer experiences while achieving operational excellence through modern, scalable, and secure technology architecture."

Business-Aligned

Every technology decision directly supports business outcomes and measurable value creation

Cloud-First

Prioritize cloud-native solutions for scalability, resilience, and cost optimization

Data-Centric

Treat data as a strategic asset with governance, quality, and accessibility priorities

Security by Design

Embed security considerations into every architectural decision and implementation
