Big Corp IT Strategy

Transforming Technology to Drive Business Excellence | 2025-2028

Executive Summary

Big Corp's IT strategy focuses on modernizing our technology foundation to support aggressive growth targets while reducing operational costs by 15%. Through strategic investments in cloud infrastructure, data capabilities, and automation, we will enable new revenue streams worth $50M annually while improving customer satisfaction by 25%. This three-year transformation requires $12M investment and will position Big Corp as a technology leader in our industry.
